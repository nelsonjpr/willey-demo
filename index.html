FAQ

Questions, answered

What kinds of knives do you sharpen?

Kitchen, pocket, hunting and outdoor knives. We don't sharpen lawnmower blades. Full sharpening details.

How long does shipping take?

See our shipping policy for current times. Orders over $149 ship free.

How do I pay?

Once your knives arrive and are sharpened, we call you to take payment and then ship them back.

Can you fix a broken tip?

Minor tip breaks and nicks, yes. For bigger damage on brands we carry, we'll point you to the manufacturer's repair process.

Can I bring them to the shop instead?

Absolutely. Call (302) 349-4070 to book an appointment and wait while we sharpen.
